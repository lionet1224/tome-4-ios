ToME Radial Layout

Second proposal: no hotbar, no thumb buttons. Press and hold anywhere in the left third and a wheel of your hotkeys opens under the thumb; slide to one and let go. The right third opens a wheel with explore, rest, inventory and the rest. A plain tap anywhere is still walk, attack, or open door. Tap yourself to wait, hold yourself for the self menu. Try both "opens on" settings below.

Situations
Wheel opens on Wheel sits

Laptop: press and hold the mouse button in the left or right third, slide, release. Long-press on a tile outside the thirds = 450 ms hold. Keyboard 1–9 fires hotkeys, Escape closes anything.

What is on screen

  1. Vitals + level-upT2Life, mana, stamina. The gold + appears only with unspent points; the Character wedge on the right wheel glows at the same time, so either route works.
  2. Message lineT1Last two log lines. The aiming strip drops in below it while a talent is being aimed.
  3. Hotkey wheelT1Left third. Eight hotkeys per page as wedges, numbered like the keyboard, cooldown turns shown in the wedge. The ninth wedge flips the page without closing the wheel: slide onto it, the page changes, slide on to the talent. Let go in the hub to cancel.
  4. Action wheelT1–T3Right third. Explore and Rest sit on the side nearest the thumb's natural sweep; Inventory, Talents, Character, Journal, Menu and the two zoom steps fill the rest. No pages.
  5. Cooldown pipsT1One pip per hotkey on the current page, under the vitals. Gold with the slot number = ready. Red with a count = turns left. Dim = empty slot. This is the at-a-glance view the hotbar used to give.
  6. The mapT1Tap = walk, travel, attack, open door. Tap yourself = wait, or stairs when on them. Hold a tile = its action menu. Drag = pan. Inside the two thirds, the hold (or the first movement, your pick) belongs to the wheel instead, so the tile menu and pan live in the middle third there.

To decide on the phone

  • TriggerHold 160 ms, or open on the first movement? Hold keeps "drag = pan" everywhere; first-movement opens instantly but pan only works in the middle third.
  • Where the wheel sitsUnder the finger (clamped so it stays on screen) or always anchored in the corner? Under the finger means the wheel lands in a different spot each time; the corner means a fixed muscle-memory layout but a reach.
  • Zone sizeLeft and right thirds, as drawn? Or only the bottom half of each third, leaving the top for tile holds?
  • Wedge count8 hotkeys per page plus the page wedge. ToME has 12 per page on desktop; 12 wedges are 30° each, tight but possible. Keep 8 and use more pages?
