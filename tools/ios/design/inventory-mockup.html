ToME Pack Screen

Three panes: what you wear, what you carry, and the item in hand compared with what's in its slot. Tap a row to compare. Double-tap to wear it. Hold a row, drag it onto a slot; slots turn green where it fits. Drag a worn slot into the list to take it off. Example items only.

What is on screen

  1. WearingEvery slot as a card: icon, slot name, what's in it. Tap a card to see that item on the right (primary action becomes Take off). Cards light green or red while you drag something over them.
  2. CarryingFilter chips instead of tabs (All, Weapons, Armour, Jewelry, Inscriptions, Tools, Other, Chest), horizontally scrollable. Rows: icon, name, one line of numbers, and a tag on the right: better, worse, new slot, or worn. Rows are 68 px.
  3. Compare cardSelected item on top, then a table: each stat with the new value, the worn value, and an arrow where they differ. Below it the item's powers in the game's own colours. The big button does the obvious thing (Wear, Use, Take off, Keep). Drop, Transmogrify and More sit under it as chips.
  4. GesturesTap = select and compare. Double-tap = the primary action. Hold a row until it lifts, drag it, drop on a slot. Hold a worn card, drag into the list = take off. Drag and drop and the list scroll don't fight: a plain drag scrolls, a hold-then-drag moves.

Questions for you

  • Chips vs tabsFilter chips that scroll sideways (shown) feel more native than a tab bar. Keep, or do you want the tab bar back?
  • Compare tableWhich stats matter enough to always show? Shown: damage/armour, defence, accuracy or crit, weight. Everything else lives in the powers text below.
  • Double-tapWear/Use on double-tap is fast but easy to trigger by accident on a bumpy bus. Keep it, or hold-then-release on the primary button only?
