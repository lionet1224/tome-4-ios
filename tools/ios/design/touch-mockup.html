Situations

On a phone, rotate to landscape to see the mock at real size. Long-press on a laptop = hold the mouse button 450 ms. Two-finger tap (Escape) can't be shown here; it stays as on your phone today.

What is on screen

  1. Vitals + level-upT2Life, mana, stamina as bars with numbers; the same three ToME shows in the left column. The gold + only exists while you have unspent points. Tap the portrait for the character sheet.
  2. Message lineT1Last two log lines, no panel. Tap the log icon for the full history. Popups that only inform ("You feel rested") never appear as dialogs; they land here.
  3. Aiming stripT1Appears only in targeting mode. Names the talent so you know what you are about to fire.
  4. ScreensT3Inventory, talents, quests and lore, message log, menu. Icons only, 56 px, out of the thumbs' way. Every dialog gets a close box and scrolls by drag; tapping the map behind it does nothing.
  5. ZoomT3Two steps in, two out, replacing pinch.
  6. HotbarT1Ten slots at 96 px (keys 1–0), pages 1–3 by swiping sideways, dots above show the page. A red dot marks talents that need a target: circle = area, square = bolt. Cooldown greys the slot with the turns left. Tap without a target dot = fires now, like keyboard with no target needed.
  7. Explore / RestT1112 px, bottom-right where the right thumb rests. In targeting mode the same two spots become Cast and Cancel, so the thumb never moves.
  8. The mapT1Tap = walk, travel, attack, open door (what a click does today). Drag = pan. Hold = action menu for that tile (walk here, look, talk, throw…). Tap yourself = wait a turn (the most common key in a fight after the hotkeys). On stairs, tapping yourself takes them instead. Hold yourself for the self menu: pick up, rest, explore, look. Auto-pickup is on, so pick up is for the rare "left it behind" case.
