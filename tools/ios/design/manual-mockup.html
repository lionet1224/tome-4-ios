What is on screen

  1. Target ring and skill stripTap an enemy: a gold ring marks it and the strip appears with your usable skills first, cooldowns greyed with the turns left. Tap a skill to cast at the ring. Tap the ✕ or the floor to dismiss. Long-press the enemy to inspect it.
  2. Drag to stepTouch anywhere on the map and drag: the destination tile lights up gold with an arrow while your finger is down, so you see the exact tile before you commit. Release to step, drag back to the centre to cancel. Hold at the end to keep walking. A plain tap on a tile still paths there; tap yourself to wait.
  3. FavouritesThree pinned skills stacked above Act. Tap fires at the current target, or the nearest enemy if none. Hold a favourite to aim by hand, or drag it onto an enemy. Hold and drag between slots to reorder; set them from the Talents screen ("Put on dock").
  4. Act and AutoUnchanged. Act shows the next step of the routine; Auto keeps going until a stop rule fires.
  5. More (⋯)One button for everything the right wheel used to hold: inventory, character, talents, journal, map, rest, options. It opens the same action sheet the inventory uses.
  6. Cooldown stripStays under the minimap, only showing what is cooling.

Questions for you

  • Strip positionBottom, between the thumbs (shown), or along the right edge above the dock so the right thumb never crosses the screen?
  • Drag vs padDrag-with-preview is shown. The alternative is a fixed eight-wedge pad in the left thumb zone (about 80 px per wedge): more precise still, but it takes permanent screen space. Which feels safer to you?
  • Favourites countThree fits comfortably; four is possible if the dock shrinks to 76 px buttons.
  • Casting without a targetTapping a favourite with no enemy in view: aim by hand, or do nothing with a hint? The mock does the latter.
