ToME New Character

Character creation as a five-step walk instead of one dense form: who you are, your race, your class, how hard, then a summary and one big Start. Every choice is a card with a one-line pitch; tap it and the right pane explains it in the game's own words with the stat changes. The rail on the left shows where you are and lets you jump back. What is on screen

  1. RailThe five steps with what you picked under each. Green tick when done, gold ring for the current one, dim until reachable. Tap a done step to change it.
  2. WhoName with a dice button for a random one (the keyboard only appears when you tap the field), sex, and a strip of portraits (the game's cosmetic choices, e.g. skin and hair).
  3. Race and classCards: icon, name, one line. Tap a race to see its subraces underneath; same for classes and subclasses. Locked entries show why they're locked instead of being hidden. The right pane shows the full description and the stat modifiers with green and red numbers.
  4. DifficultyTwo rows of segmented buttons: difficulty and permadeath, each with a one-line consequence. Defaults pre-selected so a new player can just tap Next.
  5. SummaryEverything you chose with an Edit link per line, then one big Start. The game's stock character creation still exists underneath: this screen only drives it.
